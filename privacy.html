Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 9, 2026

The Slow Mystery Uploader ("the app") is a personal tool used by the owner of the YouTube channel The Slow Mystery. This policy explains what data the app accesses and how it is used.

YouTube API Services

The app uses YouTube API Services. By using the app you agree to the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of your data is described in the Google Privacy Policy.

Data the app accesses

When you sign in with Google, the app requests permission to access your YouTube account so it can upload videos and manage video details (titles, descriptions, thumbnails, playlists) on your channel.

How data is used and stored

  • Data is used only to perform the upload and management actions you start.
  • OAuth tokens are stored only on the computer where the app runs. They are not sent to any server operated by the app's developer.
  • The app does not sell, share, or transfer your data to third parties.
  • The app does not use your data for advertising.

Revoking access

You can revoke the app's access at any time from your Google Account security settings: https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the local token file also removes stored access.
